## App icons

The PNGs under `public/icons/` are **derived** from `sprites/eye.png` above (frame 0,
the evil Eye, 16×18 at (0,0), cropped to its 16×13 ink bounds, nearest-neighbor
upscaled, and composed onto a flat background with a rounded border). They are
generated by `scripts/make-icons.js` and carry the same GPLv3 licensing as the
source sprite.
